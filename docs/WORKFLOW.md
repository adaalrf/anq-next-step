# Using ANQ Next Step

## Following an application through

1. Add the company and role. Save the listing URL and an email or postal application
   address when needed, and keep it in Saved while preparing.
2. Paste your cover letter, record the next action, and save. You can reopen and edit
   the saved text; the letter is included in export and database backups.
3. Submit to the employer outside Next Step. Set the stage to Applied, and record the
   submission date and a follow-up date.
4. Record follow-ups to save what happened, update the next action, and schedule the
   next date, or clear it to finish that follow-up. Update the stage as it moves
   through interviews, offers or closure. Archive keeps the record and its history.

## Good to know

Stale edits produce a conflict rather than overwriting newer changes. Unsaved-form
warnings don't store drafts, and daily backups protect saved records, not unsaved
forms. Dates stay calendar dates across timezones.

Cover letters are stored as plain text. Nothing uploads documents or submits
applications for you. See [deployment](DEPLOYMENT.md) for recovery and backup
retention.
